Estate sale guides for owners and families

Practical tools for established estate businesses and the families comparing their services. Written from a builder's perspective in Georgetown, Texas.

By Joel Prentiss November 8, 2026

01 / Business systems

How to Grow an Estate Sale Business in Texas

Start with the next job your team can deliver well. Then connect your marketing, inquiry process, and calendar to that goal.

02 / Professional relationships

How Estate Sale Companies Build Referral Partnerships

Make it easy for another professional to understand your fit, explain your process, and introduce a client with confidence.

03 / Website clarity

Estate Sale Website Checklist: Turn Visits Into Consultations

Walk through your site as a seller, a shopper, and a professional making an introduction. Check what each person can understand and do.

04 / Seller intake

How to Qualify Estate Sale Leads Before Booking a Consultation

Book a walk-through with a clear purpose. A short first conversation should establish what the family needs, what is still unknown, and whether your team can help.

05 / Owner economics

Is This Estate Sale Worth Taking? A Job-Cost and Minimum-Fee Worksheet

A full calendar does not tell you what each job earns. Put the work, the fee structure, and the downside case on one sheet before quoting.

06 / Customer follow-up

How to Ask for Estate Sale Reviews Without Review Gating

An honest invitation should not depend on whether you expect praise. Set a consistent service milestone, offer the same review route, and keep support open to everyone.

07 / For families

Estate Sale Commissions and Extra Fees: Compare What You Actually Keep

A percentage is one part of the proposal. Put the included work, minimum charges, extra costs, and expected seller proceeds beside one another before deciding.

08 / Closeout records

What Should an Estate Sale Payout Statement Include?

A family should be able to follow the money from the sale records to the payment. Agree the reporting detail in advance, then make every deduction and remaining amount traceable.
