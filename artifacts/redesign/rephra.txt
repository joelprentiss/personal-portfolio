Illustrative example
Goal: Set a boundary
What you want to say

So my buddy keeps asking me to go out drinking with him every weekend and like I used to be down for that but I just don’t really want to anymore. I’ve got a lot going on with work and my family and honestly I kinda feel like I’ve outgrown doing that every weekend ya know? But I don’t want him to think I’m judging him or that I don’t wanna hang out with him anymore. I just need him to understand that I’m not gonna be going out like that all the time, but I’d still be down to do something else together.

Suggested message

I’m not going to go out drinking every weekend anymore. I’ve got a lot going on with work and family, and I feel like I’ve outgrown doing that so often. I’m not judging you, and I still want to hang out, just not like that all the time.

The condition stays clear. Review and edit the suggestion before using it.
